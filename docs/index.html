Welcome to my Loc Converter!

Hi, I'm richterXCVI, and I created this tool because I'm new to Hackmud and JavaScript. While I'm more familiar with C#, I decided to build this app to improve my coding skills. Plus, I was tired of manually copying and pasting hundreds of locations into a notepad and then transferring them one by one to my cracker during a hack...

How To Use

  • Copy all your locs (one per line) and paste them into the "Loc" box.
  • In the "Prefix" box, enter your script prefix (e.g., richter.t1cracker).
  • Click the "Convert" button and watch the magic happen!
  • Result: richter.t1cracker{t:#s.uknown_jr_vhslsk.pub_a8rmp9}

Both the "Loc" and "Results" boxes will auto-scroll if you add enough locations, so don't worry about running out of room. I hope you enjoy and find this app useful. It was super simple to make, and I love this game so much that I can't wait to learn and keep building bigger and better stuff. Until next time, folks...

Happy Hacking, and HACK THE PLANET!
